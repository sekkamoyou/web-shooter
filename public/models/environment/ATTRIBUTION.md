# Environment models

Downloaded 2026-09-07 from the original Poly Haven 1K glTF packages. All files checked against the provider's MD5 values during acquisition. Licensed CC0 1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ . Official policy: https://polyhaven.com/license . Local files are unmodified; runtime clones, scales and places the models outside the player's movement bounds along the perimeter.

| Local directory | Model / creator | Original source |

`model.gltf` is the renamed original 1K glTF; its `.bin` and `textures/` dependencies retain their original names. Metadata and file links: `https://api.polyhaven.com/files/{asset_id}`. No provider previews are used in the game. Structural walls, training targets, painted markings and container surrounds are authored in Three.js for this project.
